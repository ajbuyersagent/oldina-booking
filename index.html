    Choose your beds

    Most rooms are twin share — two king singles and an ensuite. Room 6 has a double bed, and Rooms 3 and 7 take two people plus a toddler under 5. Pick what you'd like and the Booking Officer will confirm it. Leave it blank and they'll allocate for you.

    Who shares with whom, young children, mobility needs, a group who'd like to be near each other. Requests are accommodated where possible but can't be guaranteed.

    Member emails

    Confirmation and door code

    The two emails a member gets. The door code rides along with the receipt — most members know it already, so it's a reminder, and it earns its place in the weeks after the code is rotated.

    Config

    Season dates

    Held on the Seasons tab of the booking spreadsheet, not in the code — the club re-dates a season each year without a developer. Edit a date here and every quote on the member form reprices immediately.

    These are derived dates, not published ones. They follow the 2026 structure — season opens the Friday before the King's Birthday long weekend and closes on the Labour Day long weekend, weeks running Sunday to Sunday. Because both holidays fall differently in 2027, the season is about two weeks shorter. Replace with the club's published 2027 calendar when it is issued.
