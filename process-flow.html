Where the Officer went

Twenty steps today, eighteen of them manual. Under this flow the Officer confirms, and approves changes and cancellations. Everything else runs without them.

Why nothing is chased

The card is taken before the booking is queued, so there is no invoice to ignore. The door code still arrives only on full payment. Every branch ends by itself — paid in full, or cancelled at 21 days.

What the club controls

Seasons, rates, booking windows, rooms, the door code and the member register all live on spreadsheet tabs. Re-dating a season or rotating the code needs no developer.

Lodge capacity 14 a night across 8 rooms — five twin, one double, and two family rooms that take two people plus a toddler under 5 — with at most two single supplements a night. Minimum stay two nights. Rooms are allocated in order of receipt, so availability shown to a member is a guide, not a reservation.
